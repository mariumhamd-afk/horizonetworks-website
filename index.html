Horizon Networks is an IT consultation and professional services company based in Muscat, working in close cooperation with internationally renowned manufacturers in telecommunications and industrial automation.

Our technical team handles the full project lifecycle, from initial consultation and system design through to installation, commissioning, and long-term support. We build customized solutions for each client because no two environments are the same.

Our clients include telecommunications service providers, government organizations, and enterprises that need ICT infrastructure they can depend on, built and supported by a local team with hands-on regional experience.

Our mission: to be the partner of choice for Oman's leading service providers and enterprises, helping them build reliable, cost-effective communications networks.
