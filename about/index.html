About Horizon Networks

Engineering technology for the infrastructure that matters.

Horizon Networks is an engineering-led technology company based in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman. We design, integrate, and deliver mission-critical connectivity and digital infrastructure using engineering expertise and best-fit technologies.

Who we are

Engineering first. Technology second.

Critical infrastructure depends on connectivity that works in the real world. That means understanding the environment, the operational requirement, the technology, and the people who will rely on it.

At Horizon Networks, we start with the engineering problem rather than a product catalogue. We assess the requirement, design an appropriate solution, integrate the technologies involved, and support the deployment through to operation.

Our work spans connectivity and digital infrastructure across Oman, with a focus on solutions that are practical, fit for purpose, and built around the needs of the organization using them.
